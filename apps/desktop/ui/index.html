Welcome to kuverta

            
kuverta puts your email and your paper post in one place and sorts both. A few things make that work, and this assistant checks each of them with you:

            
    
              
  • Ollama runs the models that read scanned letters and sort mail, on this computer — nothing is sent anywhere.
    • 
              
  • Paperless-ngx keeps your scanned post, reads it and makes it searchable. kuverta shows it beside your mail.
    • 
              
  • Your mail accounts, taken from Thunderbird or Apple Mail if you use them, or found from your address.
    • 
            

            
Every step can be skipped and done later from settings.

          


          

            
Models on this computer

            
Reading photographed letters and sorting mail use two small models, run by Ollama on this computer.

            
Looking for Ollama…

            
Would you rather use a hosted service such as DeepSeek or OpenAI? Add it later under Settings → Add model provider; what a job sends then leaves this computer.

Looking for Docker…

              

                
Paperless runs in Docker. kuverta writes its settings to paperless/ in its data folder and starts it; the first start downloads about a gigabyte and takes a few minutes.
