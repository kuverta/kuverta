# People

Mail is usually shown one message at a time. **People** shows it as a messenger
does: a list of the people you have mail with, and, for each, your exchange as
a conversation — their messages on the left, yours on the right.

![People: the list of correspondents, and one conversation in bubbles](../assets/screens/people-light.webp#only-light)
![People: the list of correspondents, and one conversation in bubbles](../assets/screens/people-dark.webp#only-dark)

Switch to it with **People** at the top right of the message list; **Messages**
switches back.

## The list

One row per person, most recent first: their name, how many messages you have
exchanged and how many of theirs are unread, the subject of the latest, and its
first line — starting "You:" when the last word was yours. Type in the search
box to narrow the list to names and addresses.

What counts as a person is whoever sent you a message, and whoever you sent one
to (the first recipient). Mail you sent from any program counts, because it comes
back through your Sent folder. Newsletters, marketing and notifications are
left out, since they are not somebody to talk to; **show bulk senders** brings
them in. Mail in the Trash and Junk is left out.

## A conversation

Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click a person. Their messages and yours are shown
oldest first, with the day between them and the subject whenever it changes.
Each bubble shows what that message added: the quoted earlier message, the "On …
wrote:" line that introduces it, a forwarded original and the signature are
taken off. Unread messages have an amber edge. Double-click a bubble to open the
whole message in the ordinary view.

## Replying

Type under the conversation and press **Send** (or <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>
+<kbd>Enter</kbd>). It is sent as a reply to their latest message, so it keeps
the thread in their mail program, with their message quoted below yours as mail
usually is. It appears in the conversation at once, and the copy filed in Sent
takes its place at the next sync.

The keys that act on single messages — archive, delete, reply-all — belong to
the Messages view; in People they say so rather than acting on a person.
